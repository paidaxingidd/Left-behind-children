# Measures

All items were answered by the children themselves in class (June 2023; Grades 3–6; four rural primary schools in Yudu County, Jiangxi Province, China). Item numbers below refer to the variable names in the data files.

## Demographics (questionnaire items 1–10)
Gender, age, grade; item 4 asked whether the child was a left-behind child (one or both parents working away from home); items 5–7 (duration of separation, which parent(s) migrated, primary caregiver) were answered only by children who answered "yes" to item 4; item 8 number of children in the family; item 9 birth order; item 10 whether the parents are divorced. To reduce the risk of re-identification of individual children, the public files give items 5–7 only as the binary variables used in the analyses (separation more than 5 years vs. not; both parents vs. one parent migrated; grandparent vs. co-resident parent as caregiver), items 8 and 9 are top-coded, and caregiver codes that do not appear on the questionnaire have been blanked. The three-category versions of items 5–7 (reported in Table 1 of the article) are available from the corresponding author.

## Family functioning — Family Assessment Device, Chinese 30-item version (b1–b30)
Li, R., Xu, F., Ji, L., & Zhang, W. (2013). Revision of the Family Assessment Device (FAD) [in Chinese]. *China Journal of Health Psychology, 21*(7), 996–1000. https://doi.org/10.13342/j.cnki.cjhp.2013.07.003 — a revision of the McMaster FAD (Epstein, Baldwin, & Bishop, 1983). Four-point scale (1 = completely untrue of my family, 4 = completely true).

| Subscale | Items | Wording | Scoring in the data files |
|---|---|---|---|
| Affective interaction | b1–b8 | negatively worded | reverse-scored (higher = healthier) |
| Positive communication | b9–b13 | positively worded | as answered |
| Self-centredness ("egoism" in the original) | b14–b19 | negatively worded | reverse-scored |
| Problem solving | b20–b25 | positively worded | as answered |
| Family rules | b26–b30 | negatively worded | reverse-scored |

Item text is not reproduced here; the Chinese items are given in Li et al. (2013) and example items (translated) are quoted in the article. Note that every subscale is homogeneous in wording direction, so wording (method) effects cannot be separated statistically from between-subscale differences (see the supplementary material).

**Item 13.** In the 2025 data-entry file this item disagrees with the 2024 entry of the same questionnaires for 202 of the 311 children present in both files and correlates only weakly with its subscale. The 2024 values are used for those 311 children (`b13_source = '2024 entry'`); for the 111 left-behind children entered later and the 113 non-left-behind children the item is treated as missing and multiply imputed in the analyses.

## Life satisfaction — Satisfaction With Life Scale (c1–c5)
Diener, E., Emmons, R. A., Larsen, R. J., & Griffin, S. (1985). The Satisfaction With Life Scale. *Journal of Personality Assessment, 49*(1), 71–75. Chinese wording as used in Bai, Wu, Zheng, & Ren (2011). Seven-point scale (1 = strongly disagree, 7 = strongly agree).
1. In most ways my life is close to my ideal. 2. The conditions of my life are excellent. 3. I am satisfied with my life. 4. So far I have gotten the important things I want in life. 5. If I could live my life over, I would change almost nothing.

## Learning engagement — Utrecht Work Engagement Scale–Student, Chinese version (f1–f17)
Schaufeli, W. B., Martínez, I. M., Marques Pinto, A., Salanova, M., & Bakker, A. B. (2002). Burnout and engagement in university students: A cross-national study. *Journal of Cross-Cultural Psychology, 33*(5), 464–481. Chinese version: Fang, L. T., Shi, K., & Zhang, F. (2008). *Chinese Journal of Clinical Psychology, 16*(6), 618–620. Seven-point frequency scale (1 = never, 7 = always). Vigour f1–f6, dedication f7–f11, absorption f12–f17. The UWES is free for non-commercial scientific research (www.wilmarschaufeli.nl).

## Not included
An additional eight-item scale administered in the same session was not analysed in the article and is not part of this package.
